Job Description: The Marketing Reporting Analyst supports the marketing department by producing accurate reports and analysing data to evaluate the success of marketing activities.

Responsibilities:

Prepare regular marketing performance reports.

Track leads, sales, engagement, and conversion metrics.

Clean and validate marketing datasets.

Build and update Excel or Power BI dashboards.

Identify areas for improvement in marketing campaigns.

Collaborate with marketing, sales, and business teams.

Entry-Level Skills:

Excel: PivotTables, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, charts.

Basic SQL.

Power BI or Tableau.

Basic understanding of Google Analytics.

Data cleaning and reporting.

Communication and teamwork.
