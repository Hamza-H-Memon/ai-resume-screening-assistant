Job Description: We are looking for a Junior Marketing Data Analyst to support our marketing team by collecting, analysing, and interpreting data to help improve campaign performance and customer engagement.

Key Responsibilities:

Collect and organise marketing data from various sources.

Analyse campaign performance, website traffic, and customer behaviour.

Create reports and dashboards using Excel, SQL, or Power BI.

Track key marketing metrics such as conversion rates, CTR, and ROI.

Identify trends and provide insights to support marketing decisions.

Work with marketing teams to measure campaign effectiveness.

Requirements:

Degree or qualification in Data Analytics, Marketing, Business, Mathematics, or a related field.

Basic knowledge of Excel, SQL, and data visualisation tools.

Understanding of marketing concepts and KPIs.

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Good communication and attention to detail.
